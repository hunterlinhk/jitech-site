Main pages and subpages

How your website's pages are counted.

Each plan sets two limits: how many main pages your site has, and how many subpages sit under them.

Main pages

The pages in your website's main menu, such as Home, About us, Products, Services, News and Contact. Each one is a top-level section of your site.

Subpages

The pages that sit under a main page, usually one for each product or service, with its own photos, description and specifications.

Example: a furniture factory has Home, About us, Products, Factory tour and Contact: that's 5 main pages. It shows 12 sofas and 8 tables, each on its own page: that's 20 subpages. Basic allows 15 subpages, so the Standard plan fits.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Something else on your mind? Ask us. There's no such thing as a silly question.

    What's the difference between a main page and a subpage?

    Main pages are the sections in your menu, like Home, About us, Products and Contact. Subpages sit under them, usually one page per product or service. A site with 5 menu items and 20 product pages has 5 main pages and 20 subpages.

    What does “basic automatic adjustment for phones” mean?

    On Basic, the site is designed for computer screens and rearranges itself automatically to fit a phone, so everything can be read and used. On Standard and Premium, every page is fully responsive: designed and checked for desktops, tablets and phones.

    What counts as a major revision?

    A major revision is one round of changes to the design, layout or content that you send us together, for example after reviewing the first full version of the site. Small fixes, like a typo or a phone number, don't use up a revision.

    Can I update the site myself?

    Standard and Premium include a dashboard where you can add and edit products and publish news. On Basic, we make the updates for you.

    Can the site be in more than one language?

    Yes, on Standard and Premium, like this site, which is in English, Spanish and Chinese.

    Can I start with Basic and upgrade later?

    Yes. Many customers start with Basic and move up as the business grows. We'll quote the upgrade based on the site you already have.

    How long does a website take?

    It depends on the plan and how quickly the content is ready. We agree on a timeline before any work starts.

    Do I need my content ready?

    It helps, but it isn't required. Every plan includes copywriting help, and we can organize your product information, photos and text.
