Good to know

Questions about the studio.

The studio is a starting point for a conversation, not a final design.

Is the preview my final website?

No. It shows the direction: layout, colors, fonts, style and motion. Your final site is designed around your real content, photos and products, and you review it before launch.

What happens after I send the document?

It arrives with our team as a new inquiry. We reply with questions or a quote, usually within one business day, and suggest a short call to agree on pages, plan and timeline.

Can I change my choices later?

Yes. Your choices are saved in this browser, so you can come back and edit them. Colors and fonts can also be adjusted during the design stage, within your plan's revision rounds.

How is the plan recommended?

From the number of main pages and subpages, plus the features you choose. Online checkout, configurators and booking are quoted separately because they depend on your products and payment setup.
